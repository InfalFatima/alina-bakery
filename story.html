Our story

A simple idea, baked with heart.

Joyful Bakes began with a simple belief: something as small as a homemade treat can bring people together and help someone in need.

🥐
How it began

From our school to our wider community

We started a monthly bake sale at our school, making and selling delicious homemade treats. The money raised is donated to charity so that our baking can support people in need.

What began as a small community effort is also a reminder that generosity does not have to be complicated. A shared recipe, a little time, and a caring community can go a long way.

What guides us
What we believe

Kindness is best when it is shared.

We bring people together through fresh baking, thoughtful service, and a commitment to giving back.
